Motion Analysis

Here, are the different Motion Types explained! Some of the explanations are short, but most of them should be intuitive enough to understand

This House Would...

This is a POLICY MOTION, based on whether or not we should implement/enact a certain policy.

Usually, the one implementing the policy is the government unless stated otherwise.

This House Believes that X should...

This is a POLICY MOTION, whether or not X should do Y or a certain action.

Government/Proposition teams can propose how Y would be done.

This House Believes That...

This is a VALUE JUDGEMENT MOTION, whether or not a statement is true.

It is about whether something is TRUE/FALSE.

This House Supports/Opposes...

Teams must SUPPORT or OPPOSE X based on how it manifests in the world.

This House Prefers...

Teams defend a comparison like X vs Y or X vs status quo.

This House Prefers a world in which...

Government defends world with X, Opposition defends without X.

This House Regrets...

Government argues the world would be better without X.

This House Predicts...

Focus is on whether X will happen — not whether it's good.
